latency is 1.132 versus 1.561 seconds, descriptive timing under different GPU/load

The failure evidence is unchanged: from 1k to 23k, path error falls
1.0003→0.1240 for BTM and 0.5236→0.1063 for flow, yet both lose all offset-100
cases solved at 1k. Lower latent losses/residuals do not establish physical
reachability, and absolute latent variance alone does not establish mode collapse.
Bad proposals, inverse-control failure and world-model mismatch remain causal
hypotheses, not identified causes.

Prioritize aligned post-CEM predictions and executed trajectories, physical
subgoal reach rates and failure videos. Ground-truth waypoint/shared inverse-head
controls would then distinguish proposal failure from downstream control failure.
Only after those measurements should horizon alignment, deterministic/few-step
controls and isolated full-controller timing guide improvements. These are
proposed experiments; none was launched. Keep flow frozen and test data unused.
