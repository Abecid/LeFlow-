### Run identity and the meaning of JEPA training here

These are the runs from the deployed `Abecid/LeFlow-` repository in W&B project
`attentionx2023/btm-jepa`: BTM `z78m0zk6` and flow `g0u3w723`, launched through
`train_subgoals.py`. They train planners and inverse heads while the pretrained
LeWM encoder/dynamics remain frozen. The repository's separate `train.py`
trains the JEPA encoder/predictor itself, with prediction and SIGReg losses;
those are a different experiment and metric family.

A broader read-only inventory at 05:17–05:19 UTC inspected the LeFlow deployment,
configured model/run cache, named LeFlow/JEPA locations and the user's default
world-model cache. It found the two pilot runs plus
`pusht_verification_20261004/btm_3072`, a two-update wiring check with three
offset evaluations. That check is excluded from all research scores below.
The checkpoint cache contains the pretrained PushT model. No separate
`lewm_weights.ckpt`, `lewm_epoch_*_object.ckpt` or original latent-planner
training artifacts were found in these inspected locations. Therefore this
report cannot claim newly trained JEPA-backbone results. The inventory is
scoped to these locations, not an exhaustive search of every server directory.

**Observed:** offline losses and rollout proxies improve while task success deteriorates or fluctuates. BTM's final path MSE is 0.11175 with only 5% primary success. At 23k neither method retains any initial primary successes: BTM loses five and gains two; flow loses four and gains one. BTM's final checkpoint also loses all five initial successes and gains one.

Shared regression cases `(episode,start)` include `(13082,3)` and `(8000,29)`. Persistent failures under both methods throughout all 32 matched primary checkpoints are `(13649,14)`, `(17889,25)` and `(1556,28)`. These are reproducible debugging cases. Without physical traces/videos, we cannot label them stalling, oscillation, bad contact, action saturation or infeasible proposals.

**Interpretation limits:**

- The observed residual compares the state after five executed actions with a waypoint ten actions ahead. Predicted goal/waypoint errors precede CEM changes to the executed first segment, and use different horizons. Their relative magnitudes do not calibrate world-model error.
- Offset 100 specifies recorded start–goal separation; the proposed path spans 50 actions (five segments × spacing two × five-action blocks). Training includes spacings 1/2/4, spanning 25/50/100 actions. Horizon mismatch is testable, not an established failure cause.
- Generation validation uses 32 correlated clips from two episodes, spacing counts 20/11/1 and eight candidates. Loss validation uses 512 clips from 28 episodes. Probe representativeness is limited.
- Candidate spread shrinks in both methods. At 23k BTM variance is still 1.21× flow's. Absolute latent variance cannot establish behavioral mode collapse; generated boundary MSE is zero by endpoint clamping.
- Inverse/consistency objectives train on recorded pairs without gradients through generated waypoints. Their improvement does not prove generated proposals can be executed. The world model remains frozen.

Solver timing includes in-solver encoding/generation/inverse/dynamics/CEM work, but excludes controller preprocessing/postprocessing and environment execution. An older paired JSON field named `full_controller_latency_speedup_flow_over_btm` contains the solver-batch ratio; its name does not broaden what was timed. Retained success remains statistically unproven.

Median logged training throughput over the matched 20k–23k window is 1,928.7 BTM versus 3,027.9 flow samples/second. Some intervals include evaluation waits; this is not pure optimizer benchmarking. BTM's one-step inference should not be confused with cheaper training. Recorded rollout durations sum to 6,018 seconds BTM and 7,539 seconds flow. These exclude evaluation subprocess/model startup overhead and include different cycle counts. Evaluation dominates observed campaign duration; rank-zero evaluation leaves peers waiting in collectives. Controlled timing and any future parallel evaluator need their own experiment with identical success cases and synchronized measurement.

## 6. Prioritized debugging and improvement experiments

| Priority | Evidence / hypothesis | Discriminating measurement | Decision enabled |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Flow interruption leaves comparison incomplete | On authorized recovery, resume frozen flow at 23k; require successful exit, 23,830 checkpoint, final evaluation and genuine completion handoff | Complete budget parity while preserving the control |
| 1 | Latent proxies improve while solved cases are lost | Replay named validation cases with video, physical goal/subgoal distance, contact state, actions and clipping; separate behavior before success | Distinguish proposal, execution, contact and scoring failures |
| 2 | Predictions and observations differ in actions/horizon | Save post-CEM five-action predictions and compare with five-action observations; measure physical subgoal reach within the same K actions | Separate model calibration error from measurement mismatch |
| 3 | Separate inverse heads may confound generator quality | Execute ground-truth waypoints; compare generators through a shared fixed inverse head/controller | Separate proposals from inverse-control limitations |
| 4 | Planning horizon may not match goal separation | Controlled spacing/horizon comparison; broader probes across episodes/spacings; relative and behavioral diversity | Test horizon/coverage hypotheses before objective changes |
| 5 | One-step versus 16-step omits competitive controls | Deterministic and 2/4/8/16-step flow controls; matched candidates/controller budgets; same-GPU full-controller mean/p95 and fixed-deadline success | Determine value beyond cheaper flow inference |
| 6 | Existing flat option bundles several changes | Isolate rollout duration, waypoint ranking and CEM individually | Attribute gains to hierarchy rather than bundled changes |
| 7 | One seed and repeated validation cannot confirm a gain | Freeze method/selection rule; at least three matched seeds and identical held-out evaluations | Measure repeatability and generalization |

The current flat option still generates waypoints while changing path duration (50→25 actions), controller horizon (10→25), ranking and CEM. It is not a clean hierarchy-only ablation or direct-final-goal baseline. No experimental jobs were launched for this assessment. Preserve flow and the unused test split during development; consider objective changes after diagnostics identify the limiting behavior.

## 7. Evidence and conclusion

**Primary success target: not demonstrated. Efficiency with retained success: not demonstrated. Full experiment program: not completed.** The pilot supplies usable failure evidence and a possible inference-cost benefit, but does not support a claim that BTM is better overall than the baseline.
